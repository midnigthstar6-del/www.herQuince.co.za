Questions about our shop

1. Why should I choose your website for my quince dress?

We want to make finding your dream quince dress simple, affordable, and stress-free. We offer a variety of beautiful dresses so you can find something that matches your style, personality, and special day.

2. Why should I contact you?

You can contact us if you have any questions about our dresses, sizes, colours, prices, delivery, refunds, or your order. We’re always happy to help you before or after you make a purchase.

3. Can I get a refund?

Yes, you can get a refund if the dress you receive is not what you ordered or is significantly different from what was shown in the advertisement. In this case, you can receive a full refund for the product.

N.B

"Please note that shipping or transportation costs are not included in the refund."

You may also qualify for a refund if your dress arrives damaged, torn, dirty, or with another problem that was not caused by you.

However, if the dress was damaged, torn, or stained by the customer after receiving it, it will not qualify for a refund.

4. Can I get a discount?

Yes! We offer different ways for customers to receive discounts. You can receive a discount by signing up on our website or downloading our app.

We also have special offers for our first customers. The first three customers who place an order can receive 50% off their purchase and free shipping.

5. Do you offer free shipping?

Yes! From time to time, we offer free shipping as part of our special promotions. Our first three customers can receive free shipping along with their 50% discount.

6. What if the dress I receive is different from the picture?

If the dress you receive is significantly different from the dress shown in the advertisement or from what you ordered, please contact us. We will look into the issue and, if it qualifies under our refund policy, you may receive a full refund for the dress.

7. What if my dress arrives damaged?

Don't worry! If your dress arrives torn, dirty, damaged, or with another fault that happened before you received it, contact us as soon as possible. We will help you with the next steps and determine whether you qualify for a refund or replacement.

8. How can I contact you?

You can contact us through the contact section on our website. We're here to answer your questions and help make sure you're happy with your quince dress purchase.

9. How do I know which dress is right for me?

Think about the style, colour, size, and overall look you want for your special day. If you're unsure, you can contact us, and we'll be happy to help you choose a dress that suits your preferences.

10. Are there special offers on your website?

Yes! We may have special discounts and promotions throughout the year. Make sure you sign up or download our app so you don't miss out on future offers.
